One funny line, at most 200 characters, on any subject:

> My gym membership is $30 a month, $360 a year, or $360 per visit the way I use it.

The same $360, reframed: a monthly fee anyone recognises, quietly recalculated into a per-visit price that only makes sense once you admit you never go. That is the whole entry: `main.py` writes this line to `/output/entry.txt`, nothing else.
